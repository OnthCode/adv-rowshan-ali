01 About the advocate

Law explained plainly. Defended fiercely.

Rowshan Ali founded The Lawyers' Chamber, a multi-discipline firm of advocates, barristers, tax consultants, trademark attorneys and notary public. The firm handles matters from the Magistrate and Assistant Judge courts through the Supreme Court, and before administrative, labour, customs, VAT and tax tribunals.

Thousands of people follow his talks on Facebook and YouTube, where he explains court decisions and everyday law in Bangla. He also prepares candidates for the High Court enrolment exam.
